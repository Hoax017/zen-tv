# Google TV / Android TV apps found on most TVs.
# Format: package | level | label | description
#   level: recommended = ads, telemetry or junk, offered as a group
#          optional    = a feature some people use, asked one by one (default: keep)
#   add "+suspend" to also suspend the app (for apps the TV re-enables at boot)
# Nothing is ever uninstalled: apps are disabled with "pm disable-user" and can be restored.
